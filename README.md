## Overview

Week 7 focuses on improving the training and generalization of neural networks through:

- batch, stochastic, and mini-batch gradient descent

- adaptive step-size methods for neural network optimization

- momentum, Adadelta, and Adam optimization algorithms

- exploding and vanishing gradients in deep networks

- regularization techniques such as early stopping, weight decay, and noise injection

- dropout and its effect on neural network training

- batch normalization for stabilizing and regularizing deep networks

The homework in this repo explores these optimization and regularization techniques through mathematical derivations and computational examples, developing a deeper understanding of how neural networks can be trained more efficiently and generalize better to unseen data.
